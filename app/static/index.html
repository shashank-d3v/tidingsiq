Optimism, with context.

Happy Factor helps you explore the feed. It is a ranking signal, not an objective measure of truth, quality, or how a story will make you feel.

01

Find the signals

GDELT supplies news metadata and tone signals. TidingsIQ refines those records into an explorable feed and links you to the original publisher.

02

Rank with care

The tone score is normalized to 0–100. Positive title terms can add a bonus; title guardrails penalize or exclude certain negative topics.

03

Keep your judgment

Eligible stories need a headline, a source link, and a Happy Factor of at least 65, and must pass the title rules. Automated rules can still miss context.

Reading the Happy Factor colours

Badge colours use soft amber and sage tones, with stronger green accents as the optimism score increases. The number remains visible so colour is never the only signal. These bands describe the ranking score, not an editorial rating.

65–69 · Amber70–79 · Sage80–89 · Green90–100 · Deep green

Simple, private measurement

We count page loads, engagement, and story-link clicks to improve TidingsIQ. Measurement uses a random identifier that lasts only until you reload or close this page. We use no analytics cookies or persistent visitor identifiers, and do not collect your searches or the story links you click. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals disable measurement. Our hosting provider also records standard request logs, including network addresses.
